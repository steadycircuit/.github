## The idea

Steady Circuit is a small software studio built around a simple belief: complex problems deserve simple solutions.

We create focused products for people who are tired of awkward workflows, oversized platforms, and tools that make
simple jobs feel complicated. Each product has a clear purpose, a carefully chosen scope, and a strong emphasis on
being useful in the real world.

Our work spans creative tooling, cloud storage, financial trading, and tax compliance. The sectors may be different,
but our approach stays the same: understand the problem deeply, then build the simplest thing that solves it properly.

## How we build

We keep the development process close to the problem and the people using the software:

- **Start with the real problem.** We spend time understanding the domain before reaching for features.
- **Keep the scope focused.** A product should do a small number of important things exceptionally well.
- **Make complexity disappear.** The hard work belongs in the engineering, not in the user’s workflow.
- **Build for the long term.** Clear architecture, dependable foundations, and thoughtful iteration matter.

## A small team with deep experience

We’re a two-person team with more than 40 years of combined experience across product development, technical
leadership, enterprise architecture, high-scale SaaS, and web, mobile, and desktop software.

That experience lets us work across the whole product: from the first sketch and the underlying architecture through
to deployment, operations, and the details that make software feel good to use.
